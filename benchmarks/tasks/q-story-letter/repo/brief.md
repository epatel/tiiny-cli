# Brief: "Return to Sender"

A very short story in two chapters, for adult readers, in English.

**Premise.** Nils Aberg sorts mail at the night shift of a small-town post office. One night he
finds a letter addressed to himself, in his own handwriting, postmarked thirty years ago. He has
never written it.

**Must have**
- Third person, past tense, close to Nils.
- The letter appears in both chapters; chapter 2 reveals who wrote it and why.
- At least two named characters besides Nils, each wanting something.
- The story's first sentence, exactly: "The letter was addressed to Nils in his own handwriting." The
  last chapter must contain this sentence again, at a moment where it means something new.
- Dialogue in both chapters.
