# Briefing

Since 2019 the Kestrel Line has grown from 14 stations on 62 km to 16 stations on 71.2 km. Ridership fell to 1.4 million in 2020 and recovered to 3.25 million journeys in 2023, a record. The 2023 report said 3.4 million and a councillor claimed 3.5 million, but the February 2024 memo corrects this to 3.25 million because transfers were counted twice; I used the corrected figure. The memo also corrects the extension length from 9.5 km to 9.2 km. The extension cost 214 million crowns, of which the Regional Development Fund paid 60 percent, which contradicts the claim that the region paid for all of it. The fleet is 21 trainsets and will be 25 when four more K3 sets arrive in 2026. The Aldersgate viaduct limits trains to 125 tonnes and 40 km per hour, so the 131-tonne K3 sets cannot cross it, and journeys take 76 minutes instead of 71.
