# Tarn Valley Transit — Annual Report 2023 (extract)

## A record year
Ridership reached 3.4 million journeys in 2023, a record, after 2.9 million in 2022.

## Network
The rebuilt Mossgate station reopened on 20 June 2022. On 4 September 2023 the Corrin Bay extension opened, adding 9.5 km of line and two new stations, Saltreach and Corrin Bay. At the end of 2023 the Kestrel Line had 16 stations in operation.

## Fleet
The three class K3 trainsets were delivered between April and July 2023 and entered service with the extension. The fleet is now 21 trainsets: 18 K2 and 3 K3. The K3 sets have four cars and seat 240 passengers.

## Finances
Operating income was 118.9 million crowns. Fares contributed 64%.

## Infrastructure
A routine inspection of the Aldersgate viaduct between Fellbridge and Harrowgate found corrosion in two bearings. The engineering department is assessing it, and more inspections are planned for early 2024.
