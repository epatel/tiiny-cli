# Minutes of the TVT board meeting, 22 May 2024 (extract)

Present: the full board; operations director Hanne Voss (item 3 only).

**Item 2: Fleet.** The board approved buying four more class K3 trainsets at 11.5 million crowns each, for delivery in 2026. No trainsets will be withdrawn before 2027. Ms Voss noted that the new sets cannot run north of Fellbridge until the viaduct repair is complete.

**Item 3: Timetable.** Because of the Aldersgate speed limit, the end-to-end journey from Harrowgate to Corrin Bay now takes 76 minutes instead of 71. The board accepted the operations department's recovery plan.

**Item 4: Station names.** From 1 January 2025, Saltreach station will be renamed **Saltreach Harbour**, at the request of the town council.

**Item 5: Any other business.** The chair reminded members that a new chief executive will be recruited in 2025, when the present chief executive retires.
