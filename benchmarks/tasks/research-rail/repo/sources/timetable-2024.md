# Kestrel Line timetable, valid from 2 April 2024 (summary)

| | Peak (Mon–Fri 06:30–09:00, 15:30–18:30) | Off-peak |
|---|---|---|
| Harrowgate – Corrin Bay | every 12 min | every 20 min |
| Journey time, end to end | 76 min | 76 min |

The journey time includes the temporary speed limit on the Aldersgate viaduct.

The first train leaves Harrowgate at 05:30 (Sundays 07:00), and the last at 23:40. Night buses N7 and N9 follow the line after midnight on Fridays and Saturdays.
