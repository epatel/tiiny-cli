# Internal memo — corrections to the 2023 Annual Report

From: Finance and Statistics, Tarn Valley Transit
Date: 14 February 2024

Two figures in the 2023 Annual Report were wrong and are corrected here. Please use the corrected figures in all future documents.

1. **Ridership 2023.** Passengers changing from the extension's trains to the main line at Linmouth were counted twice between September and December 2023. The correct ridership for 2023 is **3.25 million** journeys, not 3.4 million. It is still the highest figure ever recorded.
2. **Length of the extension.** The extension is **9.2 km** long, not 9.5 km. The figure of 9.5 km included the sidings at Corrin Bay depot, which are not part of the passenger line.

All other figures in the report stand.
