# Tarn Valley Transit — Annual Report 2021 (extract)

## Two difficult years
The pandemic cut ridership to 1.4 million journeys in 2020. Recovery during 2021 was steady, and the year ended with 2.2 million journeys.

## Mossgate rebuild
Mossgate station closed on 11 January 2021 for its full rebuild. While it is closed, trains pass through without stopping and a replacement bus links Mossgate with Fellbridge. During the closure the line therefore has 13 stations in operation. The new Mossgate, with step-free access and a longer platform, is expected to reopen in mid-2022.

## Corrin Bay extension approved
In March 2021 the regional council approved the southern extension from Linmouth to Corrin Bay. It will add two stations, Saltreach and Corrin Bay. Construction started in September 2021.

## Fleet
The K2 refurbishment programme was completed in November 2021. Three new class K3 trainsets have been ordered for delivery in 2023, ahead of the extension's opening.

## People
TVT employed 389 people at the end of 2021. Operations director Hanne Voss led the pandemic timetable changes, which were praised by the passenger council.
