# Kestrel Staff News — Summer 2024

Welcome to our 23 new colleagues, most of them at the new Corrin Bay depot. TVT now employs 412 people.

The summer party will be held at Saltreach on 17 August. Bring your families!

Driver training on the K3 sets continues: 61 of our 88 drivers are now certified on K3. Remember that K3 sets must not be rostered over the Aldersgate viaduct while engineering note EN-2024-07 is in force.

Congratulations to the Mossgate station team, who won the region's "Station of the Year" award.
