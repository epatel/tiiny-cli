# Press release — Kestrel Line reaches Corrin Bay

Linmouth, 4 September 2023. Passenger trains ran to Corrin Bay for the first time today. The extension cost 214 million crowns: Tarn Valley Transit paid 40% from its own capital budget, and the Regional Development Fund paid the rest.

Trains now run from Harrowgate to Corrin Bay every 12 minutes at peak times and every 20 minutes off-peak. The two new stations, Saltreach and Corrin Bay, have park-and-ride sites with 450 spaces in total.

"This is the biggest investment in the valley's railway in sixty years," said council leader Ruth Amsel at the opening ceremony.
