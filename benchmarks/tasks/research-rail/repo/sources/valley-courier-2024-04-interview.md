# Valley Courier, 9 April 2024 — "The line that grew up"

*An interview with councillor Petter Lund (Linmouth)*

Councillor Lund is proud of the extension. "Ridership topped 3.5 million in 2023," he told us, "and that's before the summer tourists have discovered Corrin Bay." He believes the line should go on to Westhaven: "Another twenty kilometres and we would connect the whole coast."

Asked about the slower journeys since March, he blamed "years of neglect" of the viaduct, and said the new K3 trains were "the best in the country — they run everywhere on the line without trouble."

The councillor also said that the extension was "paid for entirely by the region." TVT declined to comment on the interview.
