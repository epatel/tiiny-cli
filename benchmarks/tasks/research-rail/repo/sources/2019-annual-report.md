# Tarn Valley Transit — Annual Report 2019 (extract)

## The Kestrel Line in 2019
Tarn Valley Transit (TVT) operates the Kestrel Line, the valley's only passenger railway. The line runs 62 km from Harrowgate in the north to Linmouth in the south and serves 14 stations. Trains run from 05:30 to 00:15 on weekdays and from 07:00 on Sundays.

Annual ridership reached 3.1 million journeys in 2019, up from 2.9 million in 2018. The busiest station remains Harrowgate Central (about 21% of all boardings), followed by Mossgate and Fellbridge.

## Fleet
The fleet consists of 18 class K2 electric trainsets, delivered between 2004 and 2007. Each K2 set has three cars, seats 182 passengers and has a design speed of 120 km/h. A mid-life refurbishment programme (new seating, passenger information screens) started in October 2019 and will continue through 2021.

## Finances
Operating income was 96.4 million crowns, of which fares contributed 61%. The regional council's operating grant was 37.0 million crowns.

## Outlook
TVT and the regional council continue to study a southern extension from Linmouth towards Corrin Bay. A decision is expected in 2020. Mossgate station, opened in 1961, needs a full rebuild; work is planned to start in 2021.
