# Engineering note EN-2024-07: Aldersgate viaduct

Status: in force from 18 March 2024 until further notice.

Follow-up inspections of the Aldersgate viaduct confirmed corrosion damage to two bearings. Until they are replaced (planned for 2026), these limits apply to the viaduct section:

- Maximum speed: 40 km/h (normally 100 km/h).
- Maximum train weight on the viaduct: **125 tonnes**.

Weights in service (fully loaded): class K2 sets weigh 118 tonnes, and class K3 sets weigh 131 tonnes.

Rosters must make sure that only trains within the weight limit are scheduled over the viaduct. Services operated by trains that exceed the limit must turn back at Fellbridge, or run as a different set.
