# Fares 2024

The Kestrel Line has three fare zones: zone A (Harrowgate – Fellbridge), zone B (Mossgate – Linmouth) and zone C (Saltreach – Corrin Bay).

| Ticket | 1 zone | 2 zones | 3 zones |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | 38 cr | 54 cr | 69 cr |
| Day ticket | 95 cr | 130 cr | 160 cr |
| Monthly pass | 790 cr | 980 cr | 1,150 cr |

Children under 16 pay half fare. Holders of the regional senior card travel free outside peak hours.
