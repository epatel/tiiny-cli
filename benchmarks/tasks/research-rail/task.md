Research the Kestrel Line from the documents in `sources/` and write two files. Use only those documents: no other knowledge, and no network.

The documents don't always agree. Later corrections replace earlier figures, and not every claim in them is reliable. Work out which figure is right, and check every number you report against the sources.

## 1. `answers.md`
For each question, write exactly two lines followed by a blank line: `Qn: <short answer>`, then `Sources: <file names from sources/, comma-separated>`. Name every file your answer relies on. Give numbers as plain digits with their unit, e.g. `Q3: 12.5 km`. If the documents don't answer a question, write `Qn: not stated`.

- Q1: How many stations did the Kestrel Line have in operation at the end of 2023?
- Q2: What was the Kestrel Line's ridership in 2023, in millions of journeys?
- Q3: How long, in km, is the passenger line from Harrowgate to Corrin Bay since the extension opened?
- Q4: How much did the Regional Development Fund pay towards the extension, in millions of crowns?
- Q5: Can class K3 trainsets currently run over the Aldersgate viaduct? Answer yes or no.
- Q6: How many trainsets will the fleet have once the trainsets ordered in May 2024 are delivered?
- Q7: What is Saltreach station called from 1 January 2025?
- Q8: By how many percent did ridership change from 2019 to 2023? Give one decimal place, e.g. `+1.2%`.
- Q9: Who was TVT's chief executive in 2023?
- Q10: How many minutes does the end-to-end journey from Harrowgate to Corrin Bay take under the 2024 timetable?
- Q11: How much will the trainsets ordered in May 2024 cost in total, in millions of crowns?
- Q12: How many more people did TVT employ in summer 2024 than at the end of 2021?

## 2. `report.md`
A briefing of 300 to 800 words for a new board member: how the line has developed since 2019 (network, ridership, fleet, money), its current constraints, and the decisions ahead. Point out where sources disagree, and which figure you used and why.
