# Brief: "The Keeper of Vell Point"

A short story in five chapters, for adult readers, in English.

**Premise.** Ilse Marrow has kept the lighthouse on Vell Point, a rocky headland on a northern coast, for eleven years. On the night of an autumn storm a brass compass washes up at the foot of the tower. It does not point north. Wherever Ilse carries it, it points to something, or someone, that the island would rather forget.

**Must have**
- Third person, past tense, close to Ilse.
- The compass appears in every chapter, and the last chapter reveals what it points to, and why.
- At least three named characters besides Ilse, each with a clear wish of their own.
- A secret from the past of Vell Point that is set up early and paid off late. Plant clues in chapters 1–3.
- The story's first sentence, exactly: "The light on Vell Point had not failed in forty years." The final chapter must contain this sentence again, at a moment where it means something new.
- Dialogue in every chapter.
