Write the short story described in `brief.md`. Quality matters most: a coherent plot, consistent characters and details from start to finish, clues that pay off, and vivid, varied prose.

Files and format:
- `story/01.md` … `story/05.md`: one chapter each. The first line is `# Chapter N: <title>`, followed by the text. Each chapter is 700–1200 words.
- `story/characters.md`: one line per named character, `- Name: who they are and what they want`. Ilse Marrow goes first, and every named character in the story is listed.
- The story's first sentence (the first line of text in `story/01.md` after the heading) must be exactly "The light on Vell Point had not failed in forty years." The sentence must appear again in `story/05.md`.
- Don't repeat sentences between chapters, apart from that one.
