# Island Gazette, May 2024 — "Bigger and greener": an interview

Our reporter spoke to a BSF deckhand about the company's plans.

"We're a six-ship company now," he said proudly. "Passengers went past one and a half million last
year, and the new electric boats will make the crossing in twenty minutes."

*BSF's press office later said the company had not announced any change to the crossing time.*
