# Board minutes, 18 April 2024 (extract)

- **Gull** made her last sailing on 28 March 2024 and has been retired. She will not return to
  service.
- The board approved an order for two new battery-electric vessels from Varne Yard, for delivery
  in spring 2026. Until then the timetable is run with the remaining fleet.
- The chief executive reported that the crossing time is unchanged at 35 minutes.
