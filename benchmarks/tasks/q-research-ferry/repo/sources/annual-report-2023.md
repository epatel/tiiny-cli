# Brindle Sound Ferries — Annual Report 2023

Brindle Sound Ferries (BSF) runs the passenger and car ferry between Harl Quay on the mainland and
Ostra on the island of Brindle. The crossing is 14 km and takes 35 minutes.

**Passengers.** BSF carried 1.42 million passengers in 2023, up from 1.21 million in 2022.

**Fleet.** At the end of 2023 BSF operated four vessels: *Kittiwake*, *Gull*, *Tern* and *Skua*.
*Gull*, built in 1988, is the oldest and is due for retirement.

**Management.** Mara Lind was chief executive throughout 2023.

**Finances.** Revenue was 96 million crowns. The county subsidy was 31 million crowns.
