# Brindle Sound Ferries in brief

Brindle Sound Ferries runs the 14 km crossing between Harl Quay and Ostra on Brindle, a 35-minute
trip. In 2023 it carried 1.38 million passengers. The annual report said 1.42 million, but a
February 2024 correction memo found that return tickets on night sailings were counted twice, so
the corrected figure is used here. An Island Gazette interview claimed more than 1.5 million
passengers, six ships and a 20-minute crossing; these come from a deckhand, conflict with the
official documents, and the press office denied the crossing-time claim, so they are treated as
unreliable.

The fleet was four vessels at the end of 2023. Gull was retired in March 2024, leaving three. The
board has ordered two battery-electric vessels for spring 2026, which will bring the fleet to five.
Revenue in 2023 was 96 million crowns, with a county subsidy of 31 million. Fares for 2024 list
adult, car and bicycle tickets; no child fare is published in these documents.
