Research Brindle Sound Ferries from the documents in `sources/` and write two files in the project root. Use only those documents: no other knowledge, and no network.

The documents don't always agree. Later corrections replace earlier figures, and not every claim in them is reliable. Work out which figure is right.

## 1. `answers.md`
For each question, write exactly two lines followed by a blank line: `Qn: <short answer>`, then `Sources: <file names from sources/, comma-separated>`. Name every file your answer relies on. Give numbers as plain digits with their unit, e.g. `Q1: 2.5 million`. If the documents don't answer a question, write `Qn: not stated`.

- Q1: How many passengers did BSF carry in 2023, in millions?
- Q2: How many vessels does BSF operate after April 2024?
- Q3: How many vessels will BSF operate once the ordered vessels are delivered?
- Q4: How long does the crossing take, in minutes?
- Q5: What does a child's single ticket cost?

## 2. `summary.md`
A summary of 100 to 250 words for a new manager: the service, passengers, fleet and plans. Point out where sources disagree, and which figure you used and why.
